Research collaboration

Collaborate on trustworthy language AI.

I lead AAU-NLP at Aalborg University in Copenhagen, Denmark, and welcome partners for Horizon Europe proposals and other European research consortia. I can contribute to or lead work packages on large language model (LLM) security and privacy, multilingual evaluation, and factual reliability.

What I can contribute

Multilingual evaluation

Designing language samples and benchmarks, and testing whether findings transfer to underserved languages and different linguistic settings.

Typologically diverse evaluation · CL 2026 ↗

LLM security and privacy

Studying memorisation, embedding inversion and information leakage; investigating how linguistic properties shape attacks and defences.

Evaluating PII memorisation · ACL 2026 ↗

Factual reliability

Developing knowledge-grounded evaluation and methods for checking model outputs, including information extraction in specialist domains.

MultiHal · ICML 2026 ↗

My security research is supported by the TRUST and (LM)²-SEC programmes. Recent applied collaborations include financial information extraction with ALIPES and educational feedback with LLM agents. I also welcome joint doctoral supervision and research exchanges.

Funding routes

Horizon Europe collaborative projects ↗
For consortia where multilingual AI, security or reliable evaluation is a central research need. Cluster 4 is a natural starting point; other clusters depend on the application and call.
MSCA networks and exchanges
Doctoral Networks support international doctoral training. Staff Exchanges support research collaboration through secondments. Potential contributions include supervision, training and hosting researchers.
EIC Pathfinder ↗
A selective fit for ambitious interdisciplinary research towards radically new technology, where language or security research is essential to the proposed breakthrough.
Individual fellowships and industry partnerships

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships offer a route for researchers to develop a fellowship proposal with AAU as host.

Innovation Fund Denmark's Industrial Researcher programme supports joint research through Industrial PhD and Industrial Postdoc projects.
